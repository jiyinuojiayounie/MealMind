# Development catalog practicality audit

This desk review covers all 20 v3 records and six new v4 drafts. ID suffixes below identify stable recipe IDs. The original 20 retain their family IDs. All are single-serving formulations by metadata. Gram amounts target the edible portion in the listed raw, dry, cooked or ready-to-use state. A piece count carries no gram conversion. Equipment and heat tasks are inferred from the written steps, and active effort is a qualitative judgment. Elapsed time includes recorded preparation, cooking and waiting. Independent dishes are ranked using their sequential total. Overlapping steps inside a recipe are described only when its instructions support them, and no shared equipment, concurrent cooking or cleanup savings are assumed across dishes.

## Original 20 recipes

**Roles** are P protein, S staple and V vegetable. B, L, D and K denote breakfast, lunch, dinner and snack. Time is minutes. Ingredient amounts refer to main edible ingredients; the versioned catalog lists every seasoning, process-water requirement and step. Each row also calls out the highest-priority human check. Ingredient-based vegetarian tags are provisional and cannot satisfy a verified dietary restriction. No allergen absence, nutrition adequacy, equipment timing, taste or tolerability was independently reviewed.

No original recipes were removed or renamed. Version 4 records equipment and heated components for the 20 existing recipes, clarifies the quick oat sequence and rice rest, and records the prepared inputs for shrimp rice. All 26 remain development drafts. New breakfast families fill no-cook, ready accompaniment and one-pot slots. Oat stew, pre-cooked noodle stir-fry and chickpea stew add complete meals with different staples or preparation. Pantry reuse is allowed. Family IDs and method diversity discourage repeating the same dish, and Replan uses the current accepted plan as a soft family hint while preserving locked meals.

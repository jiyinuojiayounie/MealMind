# Recipe release candidate 01

Review packet for [catalog v3](../data/recipe_catalog/catalog-v3.json), based on the 12 recipes selected in [batch review 01](RECIPE_REVIEW_BATCH_01.md). All 20 catalog records remain drafts. The 12 selected records have revised instructions and serving descriptions. The other eight records are unchanged.

Quantities and phase times below are carried-forward estimates for one intended serving. Gram quantities now refer to edible portions in the revised instructions where trimming matters. Record whether each trial actually uses that basis. Process-water quantities refer to water used for cooking or rinsing and discarded. Ordinary washing water is outside the recorded process-water amount. A zero or absent waiting estimate does not establish that the recipe has no necessary waits.

The heat labels reflect the listed draft ingredients and steps. Flavors and heat remain provisional, including recipes that list no chili. The internal engine requires a verified non-spicy profile with review evidence when the request excludes spicy food. Compound ingredients, brand variations and cross-contact still require separate review. A flavor label conveys no allergen claim.

## Approval checklist for all 12 cards

- [ ] Cook the exact catalog revision. Record starting food states, equipment, process water actually used, and any ingredient or step changes. Check that the Chinese and English instructions agree.
- [ ] Record ingredient quantities and edible weights, cooked yield, and a usable serving description. Check the listed meal roles against the actual dish. Confirm breakfast or snack scaling separately where applicable.
- [ ] Time hands-on preparation, cooking from the declared starting point, mandatory waits and total elapsed time. Include relevant preheating, boil-up and reheating. Record assumptions such as existing cooked rice or thawed seafood. Revise estimates before relying on a hard time limit.
- [ ] Taste the exact formulation and record heat and flavor evidence with reviewer, date, and any product-specific conditions. Keep taste evidence distinct from allergen evidence.
- [ ] Record authorship and permission for reuse independently of culinary review. Review compound products, dietary and allergen claims, and cross-contact separately. Keep unsubstantiated claims unknown.
- [ ] Record the recipe ID, tested revision, evidence references, reviewer and date, and an outcome of revise, hold, or approve for consideration. Release approval requires a separate explicit decision. Nothing in this packet changes catalog status.

## Catalog diversity and next additions

The draft catalog still has 6 breakfast, 18 lunch, 18 dinner, and 4 snack entries, with overlapping categories. The lunch and dinner pools contain the same recipes. Only three drafts have all three lunch or dinner roles. No breakfast draft carries both the staple and protein roles by itself. The existing soups are useful as sides or, for the tofu noodle soup, as a combination meal.
